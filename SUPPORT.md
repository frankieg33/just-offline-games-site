# Just Offline Games — Support

Quick answers to common questions, plus how to get in touch.

## Getting started

1. Open the Just Offline Games App.

## My device

- Just Offline Games runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later.
- All features work offline.

## FAQ

### Which games are free?

Ludo, Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, Solitaire, and Snake. The rest open with the one-time unlock.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Restore purchase, or the Restore button on the unlock sheet. Restoring needs an internet connection because it goes through the App Store. Playing never does.

### My family member doesn't have the unlock.

The family organizer has to turn on Purchase Sharing in Family Sharing. Once it is on, the unlock appears on their device after a restore.

### Is there online multiplayer?

No. Pass-and-play on one device, Nearby Play between devices in the same room, and bots. Nearby Play never uses the internet.

### How do I hide a game from my child?

Long-press the game on the shelf and choose Hide. Unhide in Settings → Unhide games. Set a parental PIN there so the games stay hidden.

### I forgot the parental PIN.

Use the one-time recovery code shown when you set the PIN. Without it, the last resort is the destructive reset described in Settings.

### Why does the purchase need internet if the games are offline?

Buying and restoring go through the App Store. Gameplay never touches the network.

## Contact

Email **justofflinegamessupport@gmail.com**.

If you've found a bug, please include:

- Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).
- Your device model.
- Which game you were in with what settings.
- A short description of what you expected vs. what happened.

A reply usually goes out within a few days.
