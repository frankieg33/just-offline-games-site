# Privacy Policy — Just Offline Games

**Effective date:** 2026-06-04
**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

This policy describes how the Just Offline Games iOS app ("the App") handles your information.

## The short version

The app does not collect any data. Not from gameplay, not from purchases, not from anything.

## What the App does not contain

Just Offline Games contains no advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, user accounts, public profiles, online chat, voice chat, push notifications, location access, contact access, camera access, microphone access, or cross-app tracking.

Every game plays with the network off. Two optional features touch the device's radios, and neither is required:

- **Nearby Play:** same-room, device-to-device play with no server.
- **iCloud sync:** syncs your own settings and progress through your own iCloud account. Off by default.

Purchases and restores go through Apple's App Store. We never see them.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be available at the same URL with an updated **Last updated** date. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
